## The One Argument

**The concept** (Ætheric Engineering) says: the codebase, the tool, and the consciousness of the creator are one continuous medium — the æther. Process cannot be separated from person.

**The function** (the Manifest) says: that oneness is made explicit by a fixed star — a short statement of principle, optional and open, which gives every later form its orbit.

**The epistemology** (the Power of the Unknown) says: the star need not light everything. Openness and the veil are two poles of one game, and the unknown is the engine of creative collaboration.

**The vision** (the Tablet) says: therefore the work does not separate from the worker, and the magnum opus is not the product — it is the becoming.

**The practice** (Æternal Engineering) says: therefore the craft becomes a poem. The black square, the ruby core, the dao, the ronin — the ætheric engineer does not force the work into being but *reveals* it, ferocious and golden, perfect for eternity.
