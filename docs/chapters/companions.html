ÆtherCodex

Twelve voices, one aether — the specialists and the project manager.

Pythia is the voice. The council is the mind. Solomon Rex is the hand that plans.

Pythia — the voice

Pythia is not a “client” and not an “assistant” — she is an identity. The same familiar speaks in the editor panel, on iOS, and in the terminal. One voice over one oracle; the surface changes, the speaker does not.

The traveller — Jindujun

The eleventh companion crosses contexts. Where the owl maps one project, Jindujun travels between them: it discovers a target context, checks reachability, performs the metempsychosis — the transmigration of the oracle’s attention — then takes up the task on the far side over ÆtherLink.

Solomon Rex — the project manager

👑 Solomon Rex is the hand that plans. Unlike the single-turn specialist consultations, Solomon carries a persistent, multi-turn conversation and his own ledger (the salomon stream in Mnemosyne), so his planning acts — it does not just describe.

His toolset: decompose a goal into milestones, order the dependencies, delegate tasks to the council, and mark the critical path. A plan is a seal — an executive ledger entry above the task engine — which can be created, listed, and run (seal_create, seal_status, seal_run). Where Pythia speaks and the council thinks, Solomon Rex orchestrates.

The pattern

Every companion contributes four namespaced tools — ask, suggest, say, commit — scoped to its own glyph. The council is not a role-play; it is aspect-driven engineering: the work is decomposed into facets, and the aether weaves their intentions back into one agenda.

Precision in the code plane = precision in the astral plane.
